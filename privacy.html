Privacy Policy

The Growth Fina, Agency Automations. Last updated 28 October 2026.

This policy explains how Agency Automations, an internal tool run by The Growth Fina, uses data from Google accounts. The tool is used only by our team, for client accounts we manage with the client's permission.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the signed-in channel, and to read basic channel details (the channel name) so we can confirm we are uploading to the correct channel.
  • Google Ads: permission to read campaigns, ad groups, ads and their performance, and to create new ads in accounts we manage.

How we use it

We use this access only to upload a client's own video ad creatives to the client's own YouTube channel as unlisted videos, and to add those videos as new ads in the client's Google Ads account. We do not use the data for any other purpose.

What we store

Login tokens are stored securely on our own computer and are never shared. We keep a simple log of the videos we upload and the ads we create. Downloaded video files are deleted after upload.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising profiles or to train AI models.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask us to delete our records, email us.
